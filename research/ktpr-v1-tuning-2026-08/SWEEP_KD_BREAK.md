## How to read this
- **V1→V3** traces the fragger→objective axis: watch snipers/efficient fraggers fall and break/flag specialists rise as you move down.
- **V4** is the 'complete player' setting — you need both efficiency and objectives to top it; pure one-trick players slip.
- To adopt any variation, set its `tw_kd` / `tw_break` in `weights.toml` `[profiles.new]` and re-run `tune_new.py`.
