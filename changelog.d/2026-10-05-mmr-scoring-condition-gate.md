### `scripts/mmr`: per-map scoring fit is rejected on its condition number (2026-10-05)

`fit_scoring_by_map` now rejects a per-map scoring fit whose column-scaled normal-matrix condition number exceeds `MAX_SCORING_CONDITION` (200, in `momentum.py`), or that never saw one of the features, and the map falls back to the default scoring as a thin map does. This catches ill-conditioned fits that pass `_solve`'s pivot test and whose inflated prices the `max(0, ...)` clamp in `value()` does not hide. The rejection reason is recorded in `momentum_params.json` (`maps.<map>.scoring_rejected`) and in the `rating_methodology` payload (`momentum.maps.<map>.scoring.rejected`); accepted fits gain `condition_number`, and `definitions` gains `min_scoring_halves` and `max_scoring_condition`. Additive only. Takes effect at the next `momentum_report.py` refit. Ruling and threshold derivation: `scripts/mmr/README.md`.
